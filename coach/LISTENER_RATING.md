# Listener rating: how competent do you sound?

This is the outcome measure. The script and the coach measure inputs; this measures how real listeners perceive you.

It uses the **Competence** dimension of McCroskey & Teven's (1999) source credibility measure: six 7-point adjective scales. The full measure has 18 items in three dimensions (competence, goodwill, trustworthiness). Competence is the one closest to "sounds professional". The authors note that the dimensions should not be summed into one score. Source: [jamescmccroskey.com/measures/source_credibility.htm](https://www.jamescmccroskey.com/measures/source_credibility.htm).

## How to run it (monthly, about 10 minutes of your time)

1. Record a 2-minute answer. Use the same prompt type each month (for example, "make a recommendation"), so the months are comparable.
2. Send it to **3 listeners**, ideally native speakers who work in a professional setting. Use the same people each month if you can.
3. Paste them the message below.
4. Score the replies as shown in the Scoring section, or paste the replies into the Speech Coach Project.

## Message to send

> Could you do me a quick favour? I'm working on my speaking. Please listen to this 2-minute recording and rate your impression of the speaker on each line, from 1 to 7. There are no right answers; first instinct is best. Just reply with six numbers.
>
> 1. Unintelligent 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 Intelligent
> 2. Untrained 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 Trained
> 3. Inexpert 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 Expert
> 4. Uninformed 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 Informed
> 5. Incompetent 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 Competent
> 6. Stupid 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 Bright
>
> Optional: one sentence on what would make me sound more convincing.

## Scoring

- Add the six numbers. Each rater gets a **competence score from 6 to 42**.
- Average the raters' scores. Track the average month on month, along with the optional comments.
- In this version, every line has the positive word on the right, so nothing needs reverse-scoring. The original measure mixes the direction of the items to reduce response bias. This simplified version trades that for ease on a phone. Use the same version every month so the scores stay comparable.
